# Academic Innovation Challenge - Al Agents

## Problem

- University students often struggle to balance multiple syllabi, assignment deadlines, and personal study goals. Currently, students must manually read through different course documents, extract due dates, and create reminders in their calendars or to-do lists.
- Because this process is tedious and fragmented, deadlines are frequently missed. Students often realize too late that several major projects are due in the same week, leading to increased stress and reduced academic performance.

## Proposed Solution

- Create an autonomous academic assistant that transforms a student's static academic requirements into an active, adaptive study strategy. Rather than simply listing tasks, the platform should orchestrate the student's academic life.
- The assistant should proactively identify deadline conflicts, suggest realistic study blocks based on a student's real-time availability, and act as an intelligent bridge between course materials and personal productivity tools. The agent should move beyond basic reminders and demonstrate reasoning about time, workload, and priorities.

## User Roles

**Students (Primary Users)**
Students are responsible for:

- Providing access to academic inputs such as syllabi, assignment briefs, and calendars + Expressing goals or needs in natural language (e.g., "Help me plan my week")
- Responding to clarification prompts when requests are incomplete or ambiguous
- Reviewing and approving suggested study schedules or adjustments
- Retaining responsibility for attending classes, submitting work, and meeting deadlines

> Students are supported, not replaced, by the system's recommendations.

## Agentic Al Role

The Agentic Al functions as an intelligent academic orchestrator that:

- Interprets vague or high-level student requests into concrete actions Extracts deadlines, workload indicators, and priorities from course documents
- Reasons over time, task difficulty, and availability—not just dates
- Detects scheduling conflicts and workload compression before they occur Suggests realistic, adaptive study plans rather than static task lists Integrates across documents and calendars using multi-step reasoning
- The agent augments student decision-making, focusing on foresight, balance, and feasibility.
- Students are expected to what and the scope of the platform and other users
